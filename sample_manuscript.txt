# A Song of the Night Sky
By Arthur Finch

The velvet curtain pulls away,
Revealing constellations bright,
They watch the passing of the day,
And whisper secrets to the night.

A silver moon begins to rise,
Above the silent hills of pine,
With quiet wonder in our eyes,
We trace each glimmering design.

# Sonnet on the Passing Tide
By Eleanor Vance

Shall I compare this evening to a dream,
Where ancient waters lap against the shore?
The twilight mirrors every silver gleam,
And echoes whispers from the times of yore.
The salt winds carry tidings from the deep,
A lonesome gull wheels high into the haze,
While quiet sentinels of harbor sleep,
Unmindful of the passing of the days.
Yet in this stillness does my spirit find
A solace that the daylight never knew,
A calm unburdening of heart and mind,
Beneath the vault of indigo and blue.
So let the restless ocean rise and fall,
For in its cadence do I hear thy call.

# Morning Dew
By Kenji Mori

Clear drops on the leaves,
Sunlight turns each bead to glass,
Day begins anew.

---

# The Foundry of Thought
By Clara Vance

In the furnace where ideas ignite,
We hammer iron sentences till they glow,
No rhyme can tame the fury of the light,
No meter stem the tidal undertow.
We forge our verses in the molten heat,
Until the anvil rings with sudden truth.
